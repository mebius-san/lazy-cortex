# lazycortex-specs — changed since publish

**Status**: changed — version `7.2.0` is ahead of published `7.1.0`; unpublished commits sit under `claude/lazycortex-specs/`.

## Highlights since 7.1.0

- Vision templates drop the one-paragraph cap on Design Concept and no longer demand a reference to the sibling design — a source document does not cite what is built from it.
- Section comments across all spec.docs templates now carry verbatim classic definitions with attribution (Wiegers Vision & Scope, IREB CPRE Glossary, ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148, arc42, ISTQB Glossary, Cockburn, Osterwalder) in place of home-grown prescriptions; sections with no direct canon keep their original operational comment.
- The vision's Requirements section carries the canon definition of a requirement (ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 § 3.1.19 and its § 5.2.4 construct, INCOSE's requirement-statement definition): a binding obligation on the system, not a fact about its surroundings. The constraint definition it used to carry moved to a new Constraints section in the design templates, next to Risks.
- The doc-height protocol is narrowed to its one job — the relative abstraction height of content: one universal vertical for every document kind, obligations stated at the document's own height, references running upward only (a child cites the parent it unfolds, a parent never cites documents written after it), and no per-kind content rules — the vision's own height is the template canon's business; size and section prescriptions it duplicated from the templates are cut from the protocol and from the create-asset / create-from-code skills, and the ladder speaks of assets, not features.
- Overview and README correctly list refresh-sources as a member of the upstream block.
