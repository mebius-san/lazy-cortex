- The tech-writing aspect now carries the specification-prose canon verbatim, with attribution (ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148, IEEE 830, INCOSE Guide for Writing Requirements, ASD-STE100): one interpretation per statement, one thought per statement, one term per concept, completeness without TBD placeholders, verifiability with units, active voice with a named actor, and the standards' own lists of vague terms, escape clauses and open-ended tails.
